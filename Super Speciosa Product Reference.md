## Kava line (added 2026-09; not kratom — different plant)

Kava = *Piper methysticum*, pepper-family root from the South Pacific. Active compounds: kavalactones (work on GABA — calm/anxiolytic pathway; NOT opioid-type receptors like kratom). Role: closest to alcohol socially ("two beers without the dumb part"), closest to a mild benzo mechanically. No energy side, no buzz, no euphoria. Legal in all 50 states, 21+.

- **Lemon Lime Kava Gummies** — $39.99/30ct, $67.98/60ct, $95.97/90ct. **50mg total kavalactones per gummy**, 1 gummy = 1 serving, label max 5 servings/24hr. Vegan (agar/konjac, no gelatin), 10 cal, 2g sugar. Single-origin noble kava: Kelai cultivar, Epi Island, Vanuatu, root only, CO2-extracted (no solvents), certified noble by Vanuatu Bureau of Standards. Onset 15-30 min, peak 45-60 min, duration 2-3 hrs, gentle fade.
- **Super Feels Chill Vibes Kava & Kratom Shot** — $9.99/2oz bottle, 2 servings; **35mg mitragynine + 25mg kavalactones per serving** (corrected 2026-09-05 from the product page; an earlier copy of this file said 5mg), apple flavor, alcohol-free.

Usage notes (their guidance): take later in the day for wind-down; mild tongue tingle is normal (sign of real kava); mildly dehydrating — drink water; empty stomach = faster/stronger; do NOT combine with alcohol; no driving after; label says may be habit-forming; reverse tolerance — new users often feel little at first, effects strengthen with regular use (so nightly use is fine; kava doesn't need rotation).

House rules (revised 2026-09-05): **kava is a standing evening option, not a rotation slot.** It is not scheduled in the planner's Day / Night / Late table; the Late column is reds only. Any night, 1-2 gummies as wanted, no rotation needed (kava has no strains to rotate and regular use strengthens it). Take it instead of that night's Late red, or alongside a smaller one; never stack full servings of both. More only on kratom-free evenings, within label max. Not before driving; skip when drinking. The planner, the PDF export, and both printable sheets show this as a standing line under the table.

### Instead of, or with kratom? (squared 2026-09-05, from superspeciosa.com)

- Kava is sold and framed as a **standalone** product. The gummy page gives directions for kava alone (start with 1, max 5/24hr, "most people reach for it later in the day") and says nothing about kratom at all. It is not positioned as a kratom replacement.
- The vendor's kratom-vs-kava article: "two different plants, two different traditions" — kratom spans energizing-to-relaxing, kava is "primarily known for its calming, stress-relieving properties." Complementary, not interchangeable.
- The vendor **does** combine them: Super Feels Chill Vibes shot = 35mg MIT + 25mg kavalactones per serving, "many people do combine kava and kratom." Their article adds: "We do not recommend combining these two herbs on your own. Rather, rely on ... lab-tested products" — i.e. their caution is about eyeballing amounts, not about the pairing itself.
- Verdict: **neither "instead of" nor "with" is mandated.** Kava is its own tool (calm, no energy). Taking it instead of a Late red is a schedule choice we made; taking it alongside kratom is also fine at modest servings. When stacking, keep the kratom serving smaller (kava slows mitragynine clearance and both sedate), and never stack full servings of both. Because it needs no rotation, kava was moved out of the schedule entirely (2026-09-05) and is treated as a standing evening option; "instead of a Late red" remains the conservative default when both are on the table.
