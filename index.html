What the week keeps to

  • No strain in the same slot two days running, and nothing twice in one day.
  • No red repeated within 24 hours in either direction, night to late or late to the next night.
  • The reserve strain exactly as often as you choose, once or twice a week, never on neighbouring days.
  • Your days, your doses. One, two or three doses on the days you take it, every day or just some. The rotation runs across your dose days only, and off days are off.
  • Servings are yours. Capsules per dose for day, night, late and break weeks, set per person. The shelf count and the buying math follow them.
  • A break week drops to smaller servings, greens by day and reds by night, with no reserve strain: a lighter week when you want one.

Rotation is the planner's organising rule, not a medical claim. Whether it suits you is between you and your healthcare provider.

FAQ

Questions people ask first.

Is this Super Speciosa's app?

No. It is an independent, unofficial tool built by a customer. Product names, sizes, prices and deals are read from the store's public pages; nothing else comes from the store.

What does it cost?

Nothing. There is no account, no subscription and no paid tier. Links to the store may be affiliate links: if you buy through one, this site may earn a commission from the store at no extra cost to you. Prices are the store's and are the same whether you arrive from here or not.

What happens when I click Add to cart or Subscribe?

A new tab opens on superspeciosa.com with that exact size already in the cart, and the delivery cadence selected if it was a subscription pick. You check out there, on the store's own site, with the store's own checkout. The planner never sees your payment details and never places an order for you.

Afterwards, tap log order in the planner so the stock count knows something is on its way. That is a note to yourself; nothing is sent anywhere.

Where is my data?

In your browser's local storage, on your device. Nothing is uploaded; there is no server to upload to. That also means each browser has its own copy: to move to another phone or PC, open Settings, save a backup file, and restore it on the other device. Clearing your browser's site data erases the planner, so keep a backup.

How does it know what I have left?

You enter what's on the shelf once, by container and how full it is. From then on, each completed day takes the scheduled doses off: capsules or tablets for doses on the go, powder first for doses at home. Logged orders are added on their arrival date. Overwrite any number whenever a container looks different from the estimate.

What are the serving sizes it assumes?

None. On first run it asks whether you take two doses a day or three, and how many capsules each dose is, and it does not fill those in for you. Every person in the planner has their own numbers, changeable any time under the week's slider. Stock burn, the reorder date, the buying math and the printed sheet all follow them. One capsule is 500 mg of leaf; 1 g of powder counts as two capsules and a 300 mg tablet as 0.6, so all forms of a strain share one stock count.

I only take it a few times a week. Is this for me?

Yes. On first run you tick the days you take it and say how many doses you take on those days, one to three. The week shows the other days as off, nothing comes off the shelf on them, and the buying math is based on what you actually take. The rotation runs across your dose days, so a strain you had on one dose day won't come back in the same slot on the next one.

Can more than one person use it?

Yes. Add people in Settings. Each person gets their own week, journal and saved weeks; the shelf and the orders are shared, since the household buys together.

Can I print it?

Yes. Export PDF gives a one-page sheet of the week with the rules and any standing items, and Print does the same through the browser.

Do the prices stay current?

Yes. The catalog, subscription prices and deal calendar are refreshed from the store every morning, and the planner also checks live prices when you open it. The date of the last refresh is shown on the Stock & orders tab.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. It is a plain web page, so open it in the phone's browser and use the browser's share or menu button to add it to the home screen. It then opens like an app.

What about other brands?

The buying model only knows Super Speciosa's catalog. You can still use the rotation and the journal with anything, but stock and buying advice assume the store's sizes and prices.

Is any of this medical advice?

No. It is a scheduling and shopping tool. It does not know your health, your medications or your history. Talk to a healthcare provider about interactions with any medication, and follow the label. For adults 21 and over where kratom is legal.

Privacy

There is no account because there is nothing to sign up for.

What is stored, and where

Everything you enter (names, strains, weeks, journal notes, stock, orders) is kept in your browser's local storage on your own device. It is never sent to this site's operator, and there is no database behind the page. Backups are files you download; they contain the same data in plain text, so keep them somewhere private.

What leaves your device

  • Hosting. The pages are served by GitHub Pages, which keeps ordinary web server logs (your IP address, the page requested, the time) under GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Fonts and the PDF library. The page loads two typefaces from Google Fonts and the PDF library from cdnjs (Cloudflare). Those services see the request for the file, as with any site that uses them.
  • Live prices. When you open the planner it asks superspeciosa.com for its public product list, directly from your browser, at most once a day. The store sees that request the way it sees any visit.
  • Clicking through to the store. Store links open superspeciosa.com, where the store's own privacy policy applies. If a link carries an affiliate code, the store's affiliate platform sets a cookie so the store can attribute a purchase; that cookie tells this site's operator nothing about you.

What is not here

No analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising scripts, no cookies set by this site, and no way for the operator to see what you have entered. The source is public on GitHub, so this can be checked rather than taken on trust.

Deleting your data

Clear this site's data in your browser settings, or use a private window and it is gone when the window closes. Delete any backup files you saved.
